# Nexus — Your Life. Your Ledger.

Nexus is a decentralized Web3 healthcare OS that eliminates data silos. Using a hybrid blockchain architecture, it grants patients total cryptographic ownership of their medical records, alongside integrated AI, biometrics, and telehealth tools — putting health data back in the hands of the people it belongs to.

## Core Philosophy

Traditional healthcare systems fragment patient data across hospitals, clinics, insurers, and labs, leaving individuals with little visibility, portability, or control over their own medical history. Every new provider means starting from scratch, re-explaining history, and re-running tests that already exist somewhere in a system that doesn't talk to others.

Nexus reimagines this by anchoring medical records to the patient rather than the institution. Using a hybrid blockchain architecture, ownership and access permissions live with the user — they decide who sees their data, for how long, and can revoke access at any time. AI, biometrics, and telehealth tools then sit on top of this patient-owned data layer, making the experience genuinely useful rather than just secure.

## Features

### 🤖 AI Health Assistant
A conversational AI built into the platform to help with health questions, symptom analysis, medication information, and general wellness guidance. Designed to be empathetic, clear, and concise — using structured responses (like bullet points) for complex information — while consistently reminding users that its advice is informational and real medical decisions should involve a licensed doctor.

### 👁️ AI Lens
An image-based AI analysis tool — users can capture or upload visual health-related content (e.g. skin concerns, documents, prescriptions) for AI-assisted interpretation.

### 📹 Telehealth
Integrated video consultation tools that connect patients directly with healthcare providers, removing the friction of third-party scheduling and call tools.

### 📍 Clinic Locator
Helps users discover nearby clinics and healthcare facilities based on their location, making it easier to find in-person care when needed.

### 🚨 Emergency Action
A dedicated quick-access flow for urgent health situations, designed to minimize steps between a user in distress and getting help.

### 📊 Biometrics Widget
Tracks and visualizes user biometric data (vitals, activity, etc.) to support continuous, passive health monitoring rather than relying solely on episodic checkups.

### 📓 Mood Journal
A private journaling space where users log emotional and mental wellbeing entries over time, building a personal record that's as much a part of their health profile as physical data.

### 🧘 Meditations
A library of guided meditation content integrated directly into the platform to support mental wellness as part of holistic care.

### 🌿 Wellness Hub
A centralized dashboard aggregating wellness resources, tips, and tools, acting as the hub for non-clinical, preventative health engagement.

### 📈 Analytics
Visual dashboards surfacing trends and insights derived from a user's accumulated health data — mood, biometrics, and activity — over time.

### 🧪 Sandbox Mode
A safe, isolated environment where users (or evaluators) can explore Nexus's features using sample data, without touching or risking real personal health records.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React + Vite, for fast builds and hot module reloading during development
- **Styling:** Tailwind-based component architecture
- **Data Layer:** Hybrid blockchain architecture for decentralized, cryptographically owned medical records, combined with conventional storage for performance-sensitive, non-sensitive data
- **AI Integration:** Conversational assistant (via OpenRouter) and vision-based AI analysis for the AI Lens feature
- **Auth & Backend Services:** Firebase for authentication and supporting backend functionality
